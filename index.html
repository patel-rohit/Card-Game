Install Points Game
Opens full screen from your home screen, works offline, and keeps your games on this device.
  1. Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari.
  2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Points Game appears on your home screen.
Keep your games: on iPhone and iPad the installed app has its own storage, separate from Safari. Before installing, open ⋮ → Download backup here. Then, in the installed app, use ⋮ → Restore from backup. Your games in Safari stay untouched.
  1. Open your browser menu (⋮ in Chrome or Edge).
  2. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Confirm. Your games come with it on this device.
If you don’t see the option, open this page in Chrome, Edge or Samsung Internet.
